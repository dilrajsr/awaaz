How we verify

Official sources only

The assistant may only open pages on .gov.in and .nic.in websites. Redirects to other sites are refused. Every answer lists the pages it relied on.

Known portals, then live checking

We keep a list of well-known national grievance portals (for example CPGRAMS, the National Consumer Helpline and the Cyber Crime portal). The assistant starts there, then confirms on the official site. For local issues such as water, electricity or roads, the right body depends on your state and city, so the assistant looks it up instead of guessing.

When it is not sure

If something could not be confirmed, the answer says so in a "Please recheck" note. It never invents a portal address or helpline number.

Watching for changes

Pages you save are rechecked every day. If the content changes or the page cannot be reached, it is flagged with a short summary of what changed, until you mark it as rechecked.

Was it wrong?

Use the "Is this the right department?" buttons under each answer. Your feedback is how we correct the routing list.
