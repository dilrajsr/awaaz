Privacy

Plain-language summary. [Before public launch: have a lawyer review this page and add the data controller's name and a contact address — India's DPDP Act 2023 applies.]

What we store

  • Your mobile number or email and a hashed password (never the password itself).
  • Optional details you enter: name, phone, address, state.
  • Complaint drafts, your tracking notes, watched page links, and documents you upload.

How it is protected

  • Names, addresses and draft text are encrypted before they are saved.
  • Aadhaar, PAN and card numbers are removed from your messages before they reach the AI service.
  • The AI only sees your problem description and your state. Your name, address and phone are filled into the letter on your own device.
  • Uploaded files are only available to you.

What goes to other services

  • Your messages go to the Google Gemini API to produce the answer.
  • Uploaded documents go to the Google Gemini API so the text can be read.
  • When you use the microphone, your recording is sent to Microsoft Azure AI Speech to turn it into text, and the answer text is sent to Azure to be read aloud. Recordings are not stored by Awaaz.
  • Official government websites are read, but nothing about you is sent to them.

Your control

  • Use "Download my data" to get a copy of everything.
  • Use "Delete my account" to remove your account, drafts, documents and watched pages permanently.

Please don't

Type Aadhaar, PAN, bank or card numbers, or OTPs, into Awaaz. You do not need them to prepare a complaint.
