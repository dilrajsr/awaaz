About Awaaz

Many people in India struggle to complain because they are not comfortable with English, with technology, or with government websites. Awaaz lets them describe the problem in their own language, by voice or text, and does the research and paperwork preparation for them.

What Awaaz does

  • Understands the problem and asks follow-up questions when something is missing.
  • Finds the likely department and complaint portal using official government websites.
  • Writes a formal complaint in your language and explains how to submit it online or in person.
  • Saves your drafts, documents and follow-up dates so you can come back.
  • Watches official pages you save and flags them when they change.

What Awaaz does not do

  • It never submits a complaint for you, and never says it did. You file it yourself, then record the number in Awaaz.
  • It is not a government service, a lawyer, or an emergency service. In danger, call 112.
  • It can make mistakes, especially in translation. Read every draft before you send it.

Built with

Python, FastAPI, SQLite, the Google Gemini API (function-calling agent), HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
